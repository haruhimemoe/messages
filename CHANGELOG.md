## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-11

### Added

- English catalogs and the loader: `loadMessages`, `messagesLoader`, `LOCALES`, `NAMESPACES`, `DEFAULT_LOCALE`.
- `messages-check-keys`, a command that checks an app's translation keys against the English catalogs.
